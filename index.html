Core System Architecture

Engineered for flow state, not superficial gamification.

Zenith combines minimal aesthetics with proven behavioral psychology. Everything in the interface is designed to reduce cognitive friction and keep you locked into deep concentration.

◈

Purposeful Spaces

Select tailored focus environments for study, meditation, coding, and writing with custom durations, clear intentions, and instant shareable room links.

📹

Focus Cam Accountability

Peer presence without the distraction of chaotic chat. See your partners working silently alongside you via high-performance peer-to-peer WebRTC video.

🛡️

Tab-Switch Distraction Shield

If you leave the tab to browse social media or check notifications, Zenith pauses your session timer and displays a calm HUD warning until you return.

⚡

Realtime Flow XP & Streaks

Accumulate verifiable focus hours. Earn XP for every 30 seconds of unbroken deep work and track your consecutive daily study streaks.

🏆

Focus Leaderboard

Compare real time spent with dedicated peers globally. Climb the ranks from Beginner to Flow Master and Zenith Warrior without fake clicker metrics.

🔒

Zero Friction & Secure Auth

Instant guest participation with custom names, backed by secure Supabase authentication to permanently persist your stats and achievements across devices.

About Zenith

Built for individuals who take their craft seriously.

Solo studying can lead to procrastination and isolation. Zenith turns solitude into shared, quiet momentum. Choose a space, declare your goal, invite your study group, and enter deep flow together.

Minimalist Philosophy

No noisy chat feeds, distracting stickers, or cluttered toolbars. Just your partners, your timer, and your goal.

▣

Verifiable Focus

Your camera proves you are showing up at your desk, creating the psychological accountability that makes libraries effective.

Always Accessible

Runs natively in modern browsers with zero downloads or plugins required. Share a 6-character room code to start immediately.
